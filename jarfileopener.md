Privacy Policy for Jar File Opener

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Coobbi ("we", "us", or "our") provides Jar File Opener (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

SUMMARY

Jar File Opener is designed to process JAR and related files locally on your device. We do not upload the contents of files you open, extract, view, decompile, or export to servers operated by Coobbi. The App uses Google services for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and in-app purchases; those services may collect certain device and usage information as described below.

INFORMATION THE APP HANDLES LOCALLY

Files and file metadata

When you choose a file, the App reads and processes it locally to provide viewing, manifest inspection, browsing, extraction, hexadecimal viewing, decompilation, and export features. To open a file supplied through the Android document picker, the App may copy that file into its private app storage on your device.

The App stores up to 20 recent-file records locally on your device. Each record can include the file name, local file path, file size, the time it was opened, and a detected file type. This information is used only to show and reopen your recent files. You can remove an individual recent-file record in the App. Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App removes files and records stored in the App's private storage.

File contents, original file names, and file paths are not sent to Coobbi, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Google Mobile Ads by the App's production code.

Locally stored purchase state

If you purchase Jar File Opener Pro, the App stores the Pro entitlement status and the time of its last purchase check locally on your device. Payment processing is handled by Google Play; the App does not collect or store your payment card details.

INFORMATION COLLECTED BY GOOGLE SERVICES

The App includes the following Google services. Google may process information under its own privacy policies and terms.

Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)

The App displays ads through Google Mobile Ads/AdMob. Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share data such as IP address, device and account identifiers (including the Android advertising ID where available), advertising and app-interaction information, and diagnostic information. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related purposes. For more information, see:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Firebase Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics to understand how App features are used and to improve reliability. The App logs feature interactions and limited, non-content file attributes such as file extension, file-size range, file-name-length range, and general file-name characteristics. The App does not send raw file names, file paths, file contents, manifest contents, class names, or decompiled source through these analytics events.

Firebase Analytics may also collect device, app, and usage information as described in Google's privacy documentation.

Firebase Crashlytics

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and stability issues. Crashlytics may collect crash stack traces, relevant app state, device metadata, and installation identifiers when a crash occurs. For more information, see:
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play Billing

The App uses Google Play Billing to display the available Pro product, process purchases, and restore purchases. Google Play handles payment information. We receive purchase-status information needed to provide the purchased feature.

HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use locally handled information to provide file-related features and recent-file functionality. We use data provided by Google services to display and measure ads, understand aggregate App usage and improve features, diagnose crashes and improve App stability, provide and restore in-app purchases, and prevent fraud and abuse.

SHARING OF INFORMATION

We do not sell the contents of files you open in the App. Information is shared with the service providers listed above only as part of their services to the App.

If you use the App's share feature, the file or text you choose to share is sent to the application or service you select through the Android share sheet. That recipient's privacy policy applies to its handling of the shared information.

PERMISSIONS AND ACCESS

The App may request or use files and media access to open a file you select, show recent files, and, on supported Android versions, scan public storage for JAR files after the required permission is granted.

The App uses document-picker access to read a file or write extracted/exported files only at a location you select.

The App uses Internet and network-state access to load ads, use Firebase services, and use Google Play Billing.

The App does not request access to contacts, precise location, microphone, camera, or SMS messages.

YOUR CHOICES AND CONTROLS

You can remove individual recent-file records in the App. You can clear all locally stored App data through your device settings or by uninstalling the App. You can manage or reset your Android advertising ID and advertising preferences in your device settings. You can choose whether to share a file and which recipient application receives it. You may contact us to ask privacy-related questions using the details below.

DATA RETENTION AND SECURITY

Local file copies and recent-file records remain on your device until you remove them, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Data processed by Google services is retained according to Google's applicable policies and service settings.

We use Android platform controls and reasonable technical measures to protect data handled by the App. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

INTERNATIONAL PROCESSING

Google and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Such processing is subject to Google's policies and applicable legal safeguards.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us and we will address the request.
